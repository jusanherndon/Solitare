Depends on [What do Apple and Google require to list a free no-account phone game?](https://github.com/jusanherndon/Solitare/issues/5). Store name is already Klondike Solitaire.

## Question

What English listing copy does v1 use on the Apple App Store and Google Play?

Cover short description (Play ≤80), full description (both ≤4000), Apple keywords, support URL / contact email (what they are, not necessarily live yet), and category (Games / Card). Do not use “For Kids” or “For Children” unless a later ticket puts the app in Apple’s Kids Category. Screenshots and icons are a separate ticket.

## Answer

Store name remains **Klondike Solitaire**. English only. No “kids,” “children,” or Family/Kids category on this ticket ([Does Play list Klondike Solitaire as including children?](../klondike-play-store/issues/05-play-target-audience-children.md) still decides Play’s child audience).

**Play short description** (67 characters):
`Free Klondike Solitaire. Draw-one, undo, resume. No ads, no account.`

**Full description** (both stores):

Klondike Solitaire is a free, classic card Game for phones. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Draw one from the Stock. Build the four Foundations Ace to King. Tableau builds down in alternating colors. Recycle the Waste as often as you need.

Tap a card then tap where it should go, or drag it. Double-tap to auto-move — Foundation first, then Tableau. Undo any move in the current Game. Leave and come back: the unfinished Game resumes on this device.

English. Portrait and landscape. Phones only.

**Apple keywords** (61 bytes; name words omitted):
`cards,patience,draw one,undo,classic,offline,no ads,card game`

**Support:** contact `jherndon111@gmail.com`. Support URL `https://jusanherndon.github.io/Solitare/privacy/` (the public policy page, once `01` publishes it).

**Category:** Game / Card on both stores. Not Casino. Not Apple Family or Kids.
