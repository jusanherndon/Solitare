# Who will be the 12 closed testers?

Type: grilling
Status: open

Depends on [What exact Play Console closed-testing steps does a personal account need before production?](issues/08-play-closed-testing.md).

## Question

Who will opt into the Play closed test so a personal developer account can apply for production?

Need **at least 12 people** with Google (or Workspace) accounts who will:

- Open the **Published** opt-in link (being on an email list or Google Group is not enough)
- Stay opted in **continuously for 14 days** (opt-out then rejoin restarts that person)
- Actually play the Game (Google also reviews engagement, not just headcount)
- Not stay opted into **internal** testing at the same time — they must leave internal before closed counts

Decide a recruiting plan (friends/family, a Google Group, extra backups beyond 12). Do not collect or paste a full email list into this ticket if that would publish private addresses in the repo — a count, channel, and “backups exist” is enough; the live list lives in Play Console.

Do not enroll, upload, or send opt-in links on this ticket. Running the test is [Can we run the closed test and apply for production?](issues/11-run-closed-test-apply-production.md).
