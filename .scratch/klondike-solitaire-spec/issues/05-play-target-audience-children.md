# Does Play list Klondike Solitaire as including children?

Type: grilling
Status: open
GitHub: #12 — https://github.com/jusanherndon/Solitare/issues/12

Depends on [What do Apple and Google require to list a free no-account phone game?](https://github.com/jusanherndon/Solitare/issues/5).

## Question

Does Google Play’s target audience for Klondike Solitaire include children?

If yes, Play Families policy applies. If no, listing and in-app art must not look child-directed, and metadata must not say “For Kids” / “For Children.” Apple should stay 4+ and out of the Kids Category unless this decision says otherwise. The app is a family Game; that is not the same as declaring children as the target audience.
