Privacy Policy

Solitaire Collection, published by Justin Herndon.

Information collected

Solitaire Collection collects no personal data. The app sends nothing off the device.

On this device

Game progress for resume, and Undo history, stay only on the device.

Third parties

The app ships no analytics, ads, crash-reporting, or other third-party SDKs that transmit data. No third party receives data from the app.

Deletion

Nothing is sent off the device, so there is no consent to withdraw and nothing to delete remotely. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the on-device state.
