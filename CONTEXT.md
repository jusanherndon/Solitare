# Solitaire

A free Klondike app for families.

## Language

**Klondike**:
The one-deck Solitaire this app plays: seven Tableau piles, four Foundations, a Stock, and a Waste. Cards are drawn from the Stock one at a time.
_Avoid_: Solitaire (as the specific game), Patience, Windows Solitaire, Spider, FreeCell, Pyramid, draw-three

**Game**:
A single Klondike deal from the opening layout until the player wins or starts a new deal.
_Avoid_: Session, round, match

**Tableau**:
The seven columns of cards in the play area.
_Avoid_: Board, columns (as the name of the area)

**Foundation**:
The four suit piles a Game is trying to complete, Ace through King.
_Avoid_: Home, stack (alone)

**Stock**:
The remaining undealt cards, drawn one at a time onto the Waste.
_Avoid_: Deck (after the deal), draw pile

**Waste**:
The face-up pile of cards drawn from the Stock.
_Avoid_: Discard, talon

**Win**:
Every card on the Foundations, Ace through King in each suit.
_Avoid_: Complete, finished (as the name of the outcome)
